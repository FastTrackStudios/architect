Added architect_shell::popup (Popup, PopupRow, try_use_popup, under): a view's own menu drawn over the whole window by the workspace host, at the pointer or under a button, kept inside the window, closed by a press anywhere or Escape, with arrow keys and Return. The navigator's folder, tag and property menus, its Sort and group menu and its vault profiles use it. Improved: every menu row lights under the pointer (the app's [role=menuitem] hover), the workspace's menus have dividers, red destructive rows, checkmarks on choices (the tab list ticks the current tab) and open over the sidebars on a desk, in the shell's cover.
