Islands (studio#445): a distribution links in a WASI component whose views draw live on a share link’s now-playing view (Distribution::island, architect_sites::SiteIsland); the node serves its files, the web runtime and a sandbox document from /_ext/, the sandbox its own document with its own CSP (sandbox allow-scripts, no network), so the share page’s CSP is unchanged; the player brings a view in through the runtime’s share-page allow-lists, keeps /S/… apart from /s/…, and follows a chart a third of the way down. A view's stylesheet (mountView's new `sheet`/`sheetUrls`, for every embedder of @fasttrackstudio/architect; loadExtension's new `sandbox`) is tokenised and parsed as CSS and held to an allow-list, refused whole on anything else, and the view's host keeps its box contract from outside its shadow root. Every Site's own script carries the share pages' allow-lists (`architect.rules`), so the now-playing view brings a page's chart and lyrics in on Sites without the node's runtime too. `/_ext/` answers GET and HEAD only. A sheet's `url()` is resolved as the browser will request it and kept only as the normalised path of one of the extension's own files on the page's origin (no `%`, backslash, query or fragment), `&` is refused outside a rule, a custom property holding a block refuses the sheet, a sheet is bounded in size, rules, nesting and selector length, its animations end, a reader who asked for less motion gets none, and the view's host also pins `transform`, `translate`, `scale`, `rotate`, `zoom`, `margin`, `box-shadow`, `outline` and `filter`.
