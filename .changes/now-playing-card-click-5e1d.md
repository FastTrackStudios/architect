The Now Playing bar is a share page's player card: the song's art, title over subtitle, the source's two slots and play, its progress a thin line along its bottom edge; previous, next, the position and the source switcher are its detail, opened by `^` (or a press on the song). A source asks for its slots with `PLAYHEAD_SET`'s `slots` (at most two of `click` and `open`, each once; the bar always draws `open`, so its detail is reachable from the keyboard), and an item may give its `tempo` (`{bpm, at, meter}`: bpm 20 to 400, meter 1 to 16); the web app sounds Click on the active item's beats while it plays, and suspends its audio while Click is off or nothing plays. Every time in a `PLAYHEAD_SET` (`duration`, `time`, a section's `start` and `end`, a mark's `at`, a tempo's `at`) is now 0 to 24 h (`MAX_SECONDS`); one above it is refused, and a media length above it is not taken. A `PLAYHEAD_SET` without `index` keeps the current item when it is still in the queue (a view mounted again no longer sends the source back to its first item). Fixed: the favourites' round button sat under the bar at desk width; a pane going from one island view to another kept the last view's frame (keyed by island and view); an island view's frame was drawn on an opaque box (the frame element's colour scheme is now the one its document is handed, following the app's theme as it changes).
