The Appearance page's rows are search-to-row targets: a hit on a setting scrolls the page to its row and pulses it, as the standard page does. Each row has the id `setting-<key>` (`setting-appearance.theme`, `setting-appearance.token.density`, …). Its Theme row is one row with the light and dark cards under it, where it had two groups. "dark mode" also finds the Theme row (`appearance.theme`), next to Mode.
