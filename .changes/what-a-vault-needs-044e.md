What a vault needs is an item in the status bar (a row at the top on a phone) that opens a dialog over the window, instead of a card across the page; it shows once the vault opens, however late. MissingExtensions takes bare (no frame of its own) and scrolls its rows in a dialog; a prompt past four names counts them.
