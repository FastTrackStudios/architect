Changed the iOS keyboard to come up only when asked: a tap that ends on a field (`TapStroke`, `tap_raises`), the action bar's new keyboard button (`Keyboard::foot`, `Foot`) or `show_keyboard`; a press that becomes a scroll and an `autofocus` no longer raise it, and `set_typing` only reports the focus. `hide_keyboard` puts it away whatever holds the focus (the editing bar's Hide button). Added `set_input_debug` / `ARCHITECT_INPUT_DEBUG=1`: the touches, the keyboard's reported top and the foot's laid-out edge drawn over the app (`foot_bottom`) Fixed presses on a phone's tabs and action bar going to page content hidden under them (a calendar's events below the fold): the foot has its own layer, `layer::FOOT`, and the shell root is a stacking context; a phone's drawer and its scrim are on `layer::SHEET`. Added `KeyboardTaps` / `use_keyboard_taps`: a root's pointer events and click applying the same tap rule, for an app whose sheets are drawn beside the shell.
