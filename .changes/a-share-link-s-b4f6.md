A share link's setlist plays like a streaming app's playlist: a block's [data-playlist] rows are the queue and play in place with no page load between songs, the bar at the foot (icons, the song, a line for where it is) opens a now-playing view of the playing track's [data-now-playing] (moved in or fetched from its page), with the player's controls under it unless it brings its own ([data-transport])
