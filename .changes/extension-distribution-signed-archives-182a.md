Extension distribution (#182): the signed `.archext` archive (new crate `architect-extension-archive`: a gzipped tar of an extension's files, a content index of their SHA-256, a detached minisign signature over it; `pack`, `open`, `Verified::extract`, `Trust` with the org release key by default, `ArchiveError`; `sign` feature for signing and throwaway keys; `registry::check` for an index). architect-extensions: `Location::Archive` and `Listing::{platforms, changelog}` in `index.toml` (`archive`, `sha256`, `platforms`, `changelog`), `Listing::no_build_for`, `index::host_targets`; `Fetched::Archive`, `InstallError::Archive`, `Source::trust`, `FolderRegistry`/`GitRegistry::{trusting, with_fetch}`, `Installs::{with_trust, trust, open_archive, read_archive, install_archive}`; an archive is installed only when signed by a trusted key, unchanged, with a build for this platform (`bin/<triple>/x` lands at `bin/x`); `Preview::{platforms, cannot_run, signed_by, changelog}`; `Extensions::{updates, install_archive}` and `Available`; `EXT_UPDATE {check: true}` and `EXT_INSTALL_FILE`; `Missing::CannotRun` for a process with no build here; the manager shows "update available" with its changelog line, within the pin. API break (pre-1.0): `Listing`, `Location`, `Fetched`, `InstallError`, `Preview`, `UpdateArgs` and `browse::Row` gained fields or variants. xtask: `ext-pack`, `ext-sign`, `ext-verify`, `ext-keygen`, `ext-index`. The reusable workflow `.github/workflows/extension-release.yml`; the registry skeleton in `examples/registry/`.
