A share's now-playing view is fetched only from a page the link built (marked X-Architect-Page: built by the node and a static Site's router; the builder no longer publishes a vault file named index.html or 404.html) and imported through the builder's allow-list; without a playlist the bar's song stays a link to its page. The view puts where the song is at the top, the controls under it, lyrics a tap away; focus moves in and back, failed loads are said and retried, a refused recording stops the queue, and the lock screen shows the song (Media Session).
