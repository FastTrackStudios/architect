tags: [recipe]
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 500 g minced beef
  - 2 cans kidney beans
  - 1 can chopped tomatoes
  - 1 onion
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 2 tsp chili powder
  - 1 tsp cumin
---
# Chili

A recipe for the Recipe scaler ([[R7 TypeScript actions]]).

Brown the beef with the onion and garlic, add the spices, then the tomatoes and beans, and simmer for an hour.
