# H13 MIDI and OSC

Ports by capability, and control surfaces with banks and feedback.

**Stands in for:** CSI, ReaLearn, OSC.

## Try

1. Run `Surface: open` (a virtual MIDI port and UDP OSC on localhost).

## You should see

The surface's status, and feedback echoed on the virtual port. No hardware or audio.

**Example:** Surface (`apps/sandbox/extensions/surface`).

Its status on desktop, web and iOS is in [[Showcase]].
